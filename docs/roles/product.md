# Product

Decide what to build and why, and agree when it is done.

## Skills

| Skill | Reads | Produces |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [specification-author](../skills/specification-author.md) | A request, and the repository | A specification with numbered requirements and Given/When/Then criteria |
| [specification-review](../skills/specification-review.md) | A specification, and the repository | A verdict and findings, without editing the spec |
| [decision-record-author](../skills/decision-record-author.md) | The discussion behind a choice | A decision record |

## What you approve

The scope, the non-goals, and the acceptance criteria, before anyone builds.

## Where the skills connect

A specification from `specification-author` is what `specification-review`, `test-plan-author`, and `change-plan-author` read, but each also works from a written request.
