# Skill owner

Keep the skills you own correct as people use them.

## Skills

| Skill | Reads | Produces |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [skill-correction](../skills/skill-correction.md) | A correction and its evidence | A failing eval case and the smallest fix, after confirmation |

## What you approve

Each correction's case and fix, and every change to an eval's checks.

## Where the skills connect

A correction from any role above becomes a case through `skill-correction`; [Verification](../verification.md) explains how the evals run.
